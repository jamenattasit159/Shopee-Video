# shopee_gpt

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-prescribed React / Next.js, latest Tailwind CSS and daisyUI, Lucide React. Independent local application in shopee_gpt; do not change the incumbent application.

## Users

The owner first, shooting footage of products they actually buy, producing Thai Shopee affiliate videos. Commercial distribution is a future goal, not a current subscription service.

## Product Purpose

Take a Shopee link, verified product details, original product photos and real demonstration videos through a script, shot plan and voiceover into an editable CapCut project via Codex.

## Operating Context

Use locally first. Preserve original affiliate links. Keep product evidence separate from generated copy. Persist projects and assets. CapCut account authorization is performed by the owner in the provider's browser flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The new system replaces Remotion rendering with CapCut × Codex. A successful exported package is not a successful CapCut edit: draft links and completion must come from the connected provider. No invented testimonials, sales metrics, review claims or promises of popularity. Commercial hosting, billing and multi-user access remain future decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Premium modern SaaS, dark-first daisyUI themes, Thai-readable typography, responsive accessible controls. User chose a visual mockup before UI implementation.

## Evidence on Hand

Incumbent repository has Shopee ingestion, script and voice workflows. New product has no supplied original demonstration video yet; no seeded footage may be represented as the user's own work.

## Product Principles

- Real demonstration footage leads the story.
- Save every edit and expose recoverable errors.
- Distinguish verified facts, personal observations and generated suggestions.
- Explain connection requirements honestly.
- Make the first personal workflow complete before building commercial account and billing features.
