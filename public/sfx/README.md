# Paper Collage Sound Effects (SFX)

This folder contains sound effects used by the Remotion Paper Collage animation engine.

## Required Sound Assets:
- `sfx-pop.mp3`: Punchy tactile pop when stickers, cutouts, or titles pop in.
- `sfx-slap.mp3`: Slapping sound when paper strips slap across the frame.
- `sfx-tear.mp3`: Paper tear sound effect for torn transitions or torn edge revelations.
- `sfx-whoosh.mp3`: Quick whoosh transition between scene layers.
- `sfx-sparkle.mp3`: High-pitch chime or sparkle when doodle stars pop in.
- `sfx-cash.mp3`: Cash register or metallic ding on price burst and call-to-action reveal.
